# JBeam Forge 0.19.6: the export says what would fall off

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.19.6.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.19.6-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.19.6_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.19.6.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay on format v28.

## What would fall off

The export now checks that every node of the car, as its default configuration builds it, has a path of beams to the body. Anything without one (a frame half from before 0.19.5 with the quarter panels, bumper and lights hanging on it; a piece left loose by hand in the JBeam workspace) is named in a warning with its node count: in the game it would fall off at spawn. The fix is the usual one, regenerate the part, or join the pieces in the JBeam workspace.

## Under the hood

Every button, dropdown option, checkbox and radio in every workspace, and in every window they open, is now pressed by a test on the practice car before a release (951 controls this time): nothing errors, nothing hangs, every window closes.
