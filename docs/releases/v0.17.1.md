# JBeam Forge 0.17.1: Sound as a workspace, bumpers of their own, the stretched suspension on screen

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.17.1.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.17.1-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.17.1_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.17.1.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay at format v26.

## What changed since 0.17.0

- **Sound is a workspace tab** on the toolbar, after Engine (engine mods get it too). It was only a tab in the Properties column, which was easy to miss.
- **Bumpers have a beam preset of their own** (`Bumper`): stiffer and heavier than skirts and spoilers, as the game's are. Regenerate a bumper to pick it up.
- **The 3D view shows a fitted suspension stretched to the axle's track**, the way the export writes it; before, only the export moved.
- The structure presets measured over all 38 cars in 0.17.0 are kept, with one correction: plastic panels and light mechanical parts keep their old stiffness (the game's would blow a thin skirt or a steering wheel up at spawn, since generated nodes are lighter than the game's) and take only the game's deform and strength.

## Known problems

- The latch sounds and the horn choice have not been driven in the game this round.
- Bonnets and boot lids unlatch but don't lift on their own yet.
- Parts from very different vehicles on one car can still come apart at spawn.

Please report what breaks: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Connor-Moyle/jbeam-forge---Beam-NG-Car-creation-Tool/issues).
