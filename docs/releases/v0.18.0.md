# JBeam Forge 0.18.0: the game's wheels, other suspensions per axle, two cars built start to finish

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.18.0.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.18.0-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.18.0_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.18.0.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects move to format v27 (an axle's other suspensions); older projects open and upgrade as always.

## Wheel picker

On a fitted axle, **Wheels** lists every wheel in the game that fits its hubs: 435 for 5-lug hubs, by size (14″ to 26″), brand and name, with the game's price. Search by anything in the name. The pick becomes the wheel the car comes with, its tyres follow, and the matching front or rear wheel goes on the other axle when it takes the same lugs. The wheel slot stays the game's, so the whole range of that lug count is still in the parts menu in game; before, a wheel chosen as a part option was copied into the mod and the slot took only the copies.

## Other suspensions for an axle

**Add another suspension** on a fitted axle puts a second (or third) game suspension in the same slot. Each configuration picks one, the player can swap them in the parts menu, and the fitted one stays the default. Their parts export under their own names (F2, R2…), their wheels follow the axle's wheel pick, and the track you set on the axle widens or narrows each of them.

## Two cars built through the app, start to finish

`scripts/dev/buildShowcaseCars.mts` makes two cars in code, 76 and 64 named parts each, with variant parts for the versions a configuration can pick: the **Forge Crawler**, a rock-crawling buggy with a removable top, and the **Forge GP60**, an early-1960s style Grand Prix car. The desktop harness (`--showcase`, with `--beamng-install`) then builds each one the way a user would: import and sort, Generate, axles with the Hopper's crawler live axles and the Bolide's race suspension plus the other suspensions each version picks, wheels from the picker, four engines each with the gearbox, a horn, hinged doors and lids, a tow hitch and nitrous, and four or five configurations (rock crawler, drift, trophy truck, race; grand prix, race, rally, drift, trophy), each with its own suspension, engine, sound, bumpers and parts. The exported mods lint clean and hold together at 2000 Hz in every configuration. They have not been driven in the game yet.

## Fixed

- A part whose meshes were all set to Ignore still went into the mod as an empty part, and showed up as a slot in every configuration.
- The wheel picker's filters squeezed its list under the status bar on hubs with many brands.
- A warning before export when an axle's track squeezes or stretches a fitted suspension (or one of its alternates) more than about 10% from its own width: the Sunburst's rally struts squeezed to 84% came apart at spawn.
- A game suspension whose node weights switch on one of its own car's component flags (the Sunburst's rally arms) is written out with the value the game takes in a mod that declares no components, so the 2000 Hz check and other readers of the mod see a number, not a formula.

- The 2000 Hz check (`scripts/dev/jbeamStability.mts`) reads formula node positions (the Autobello's hubs) with the parts' variable defaults, as the game does, instead of calling them not-a-number.

Please report what breaks: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Connor-Moyle/jbeam-forge---Beam-NG-Car-creation-Tool/issues).
