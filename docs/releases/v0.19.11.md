# JBeam Forge 0.19.11: see a suspension's jbeam as you move it, mirror a move, move the axle

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.19.11.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.19.11-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.19.11_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.19.11.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay on format v28.

## The jbeam follows the arm you drag

0.19.10 made a fitted suspension's nodes follow its meshes when you move one. Now you can watch it: select any mesh of a fitted suspension, engine or gearbox and the jbeam preview shows the set's root part as it will be exported, nodes renamed and moved. Drag the arm and the numbers change with it. The preview used to show nothing for a set's parts, and only for parts picked in the Scene tree.

## Mirror to the other side

Moved the left lower arm to suit your car? The axle card now says how many of the set's meshes you have moved and offers **Mirror to the other side**: the moves of the selected meshes (or of every moved mesh whose opposite number hasn't been touched) are copied onto their opposite numbers, mirrored across the car. Sideways goes the other way, so do turns about the other two axes. Opposite numbers are found by name: `…_FL` and `…_FR`, `…_L` and `…_R`, `left` and `right`, which is how the game names them. **Reset moves** beside it puts every mesh of the set back where the game and the axle's track put it.

## The axle's position moves the suspension

Change an axle's Position and the suspension fitted to it (and any alternates on the same axle) rides along the car with it, and the car's wheels and brakes move onto its hubs as they do after a drag. Before, the number was stored for the export while the set stayed where it was.

## Under the hood

Jbeam files of a fitted set carry `source: { id, root }` so a panel can find the set's root part. The harness's suspension scenario now widens the track by 100 mm after fitting and checks the exported hub nodes moved 50 mm out while the subframe's stayed.
