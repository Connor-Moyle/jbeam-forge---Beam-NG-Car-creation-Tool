# JBeam Forge 0.19.9: BeamNG.drive 0.40, with people in the seats

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.19.9.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.19.9-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.19.9_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.19.9.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay on format v28.

## Checked against BeamNG.drive 0.40

The game's 0.40 (19 October 2026) is read and exported for: the parts library cuts its 57 vehicles (the Cherrier Percheron and the European trailers are new), the game Lua the app's scripts call is unchanged, and the Skyline showcase, rebuilt through the app on 0.40, spawns all 25 of its configurations with no instability, no missing links and no Lua errors; 17 pull away under the hands-off launch probe (the others sit with the engine running, as some did on 0.39: still being looked into).

## The wheel picker and the horn list fill again

0.40's common.zip has grown past 4 GB, and listing it inside the app took 13 seconds (the zip library asks for every entry's header with its own small read, and each read answers a few milliseconds late in an Electron main process). The wheel picker and the horn list, which read the game's wheels and horns from that zip, came up empty. The zip's tail is read in one go now and the listing takes a tenth of a second; the 3,574 cut sets of the first scan load in parallel (three minutes less), and the log says how many objects and sets the scan found.

## People in the seats

0.40 gives every seat a physical occupant: a body hung from the seat that leans with the car, weighs 89 kg, carries the camera, and is how you get in and out of the car on foot. Every seat part you sort (front seats, rear seats, benches) now carries the game's `seat_occupant` slot: the occupant sits 30 cm behind the cushion's front edge, reclined 15°, held by six of the seat's nodes (the cushion's four corners and the backrest's top). The front seat on the driver camera's side is the driver's (right-hand drive when the camera sits right of centre). The driver and passenger cameras come from the occupants now, as in the stock cars; the hood camera stays. A seat with an occupant carries no collision triangles (its hull wrapped the sitting space and would have pushed the body out).

## Springs scaled by the game's own tags

0.40 tags every spring and damper beam in its suspensions (`vkTag`: spring_FL, damper_RR…). The spring scale (when a suspension comes from a car much lighter or heavier than yours) now scales exactly those beams, wherever they are in the set, rather than every beam in a part whose name says strut or coil. Suspensions from older game content scale as before.

## Under the hood

Interior cameras the export still writes carry the game's `isInside` flag (0.40 tells inside from outside by it, not by the camera's name).
