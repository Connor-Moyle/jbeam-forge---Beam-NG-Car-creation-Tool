# JBeam Forge 0.19.2: hinges on any edge, nodes moved out of the tyres, variants hinged too, quieter export

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.19.2.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.19.2-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.19.2_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.19.2.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay on format v28.

## Hinges on any edge

A hinged part's settings have **Hinges on**: front, rear, left, right, top or bottom edge of the part. Pick one and the hinge line runs the length of that edge, the latch moves to the opposite edge, and the swing goes away from the body: a bonnet that opens from the front, a gull-wing door, a tailgate that drops instead of lifting. **Flip swing** still turns the swing the other way, and a line set from two nodes shows as "Two nodes".

## Nodes out of the tyres, in one click

When a body's nodes start inside the game's tyres (a 20-inch wheel in an arch drawn for 15), the game pushes them out at spawn, bending the wing and sometimes breaking the suspension. The export warned and left the fix to you. The warning now comes with a button, **Move the nodes out of the tyres**: each node inside a tyre moves straight out from the axle to just past the tread (2 cm clear), keeping its place along the axle. One undo step; the moved nodes count as placed by hand, so Generate keeps them.

## Fixed

- **Hinge all** skipped variant panels: a Sport hood or a Race door stayed bolted shut while the stock one opened (the export warned about it on every run). Variants now get their own hinge, from their own nodes.
- The export warned "has no meshes; it exports as an empty slot option" for every part the export in fact leaves out (a model's own wheels and tyres once the game's suspension is fitted: eight warnings on a car with four wheels). It now says nothing about a part with nothing left in it, and warns only when a part with structure but no mesh exports as an invisible slot option.

Please report what breaks: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Connor-Moyle/jbeam-forge---Beam-NG-Car-creation-Tool/issues).
