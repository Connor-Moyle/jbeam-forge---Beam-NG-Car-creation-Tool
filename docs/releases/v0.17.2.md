# JBeam Forge 0.17.2: generated parts laid out like the game's

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.17.2.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.17.2-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.17.2_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.17.2.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay at format v26.

## Generated parts laid out like the game's

0.17.0 took the game's beam numbers from all 38 of its cars. This release takes its layouts too. Every part of every car was measured for how many nodes it has for its size and how it hangs on its parent, and the generator now follows that (`docs/structure-study.md` has the table, ours beside the game's):

- **Node count from size.** A door, hood, bumper, wing, spoiler, grille, light, seat or tank gets as many nodes as the game gives a part that size: a bumper went from 47 nodes per square metre to 29 (the game's is 25), a spoiler from 24 to 33 (35). The Detail slider still moves it, 0.6× to 1.4× around that.
- **Attachments like the game's.** A panel hangs on 3 beams per attached node, on about three quarters of its nodes. Before, bumpers, grilles and spoilers hung on 2 beams on a third of their nodes, which is where a lot of "wonky" came from: a part held along one edge pivots about it.

Regenerate the structure of an older project to pick this up (Generate, then Export).

## Known problems

- Side skirts still come out denser than the game's: a long thin part is given extra nodes so the game can hang its mesh on it.
- The latch sounds and the horn choice have not been driven in the game yet.
- Bonnets and boot lids unlatch but don't lift on their own yet.

Please report what breaks: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Connor-Moyle/jbeam-forge---Beam-NG-Car-creation-Tool/issues).
