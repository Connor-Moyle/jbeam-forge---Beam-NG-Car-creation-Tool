# JBeam Forge 0.19.4: more names sorted on import

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.19.4.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.19.4-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.19.4_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.19.4.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay on format v28.

## Sorting into parts

Auto-classify reads more of the names modders give their meshes:

- UK English: a **wing** with a side (Wing_L, Front_Wing_Right) is a fender, at the rear (Rear_Wing_L) a quarter panel; a wing with no side (Rear_Wing, GT_Wing) is still the aero wing. **Boot lid**, **tail lamp**, **rear screen** and **front screen**, **petrol flap**, **tailpipe** and **silencer**.
- Everyday names: **driver seat** and **passenger seat**, **driver door** and **passenger door**, **accelerator**, and Pedal_Brake and Pedal_Gas sort as pedals with full confidence.
- The head unit no longer claims every mesh with "screen" in its name (a rear screen was being read as a radio), and a high-mount stop light is no longer taken for an engine mount.
- Lights and interior: DRL, headlight glass, centre brake light, side repeater, glovebox, air vents, gear knob, rear view mirror, speakers, instrument cluster and odometer, inner and outer door handles.
- Under the bonnet and underneath: alternator, sump and oil pan, cam, valve, rocker and timing covers, starter, water pump (all engine), catalyst (exhaust), undertray and belly pan (skid plate).
