# JBeam Forge 0.17.0: the Sound tab, track width, and structure numbers from every car in the game

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.17.0.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.17.0-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.17.0_amd64.deb**: Linux (Ubuntu, Mint, Pop!_OS…): `sudo apt install ./jbeam-forge_0.17.0_amd64.deb`.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.17.0.AppImage**: Linux, any distribution: make it executable and run it.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on (Blender 3.6 or newer).
- **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game. The app installs and updates it for you (Settings → BeamNG.drive).

Projects are now format v26. 0.17 opens every older project; 0.16 can't open a project saved by 0.17.

## The Sound tab

A new tab in the Properties column, between Engine & gearbox and Configurations. Everything it sets is written into the mod's own files: the game's sounds are read, never changed.

- **Engine voice.** Which recordings the intake and the exhaust play: any of the game's engine sounds, or one of your own. Play it and sweep the revs to hear it.
- **My engine sounds.** A **pitched copy** of a game sound (above 1 it sounds bigger and lazier, below 1 smaller and revvier), or a sound **from your recordings** (ogg, wav or flac, each a steady note at the revs in its file name, on or off throttle). The newer game sounds are synthesized in its audio bank and can't be copied, only mixed.
- **Engine mix.** Intake and exhaust volume, on- and off-load volume, muffling, bass, treble, EQ, backfires, and the turbo's whine, hiss and blow-off. Each can be left adjustable in the game's tuning menu.
- **Gear whine**, down to silent.
- **Car.** The horn (any of the game's), the latch recordings for doors, hood and boot, and how much the cabin muffles the outside.

Every exported car now has a horn. It had no horn slot before.

## Track width

The axle's Track field did nothing. Now fitting a game suspension sets Track to the set's own width, and changing it stretches the suspension sideways on export: hubs, arms and wheels move out or in together. The 3D view still shows the set as the game made it.

## Structure numbers from every car in the game

The generator's beam presets came from one car, the Sunburst. They are now the medians over all 38 drivable cars (`docs/structure-study.md`):

- the body shell is three times stronger than it was,
- trim (lights, mirrors, dashboards, exhausts) is as stiff and as strong as the game's, instead of tearing off at a touch; bumpers, skirts and spoilers keep their stiffness but take the game's strength,
- glass is damped like the game's (25, not 250),
- engine blocks are 6M spring, not 15M.

Regenerate the structure of an older project to pick these up.

## Known problems

- The latch sounds and the horn choice have not been driven in the game this round.
- Bonnets and boot lids unlatch but don't lift on their own yet.
- Parts from very different vehicles on one car can still come apart at spawn.
