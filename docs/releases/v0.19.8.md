# JBeam Forge 0.19.8: a body in kit versions keeps its running gear, and weighs what it should

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.19.8.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.19.8-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.19.8_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.19.8.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay on format v28.

## A body in kit versions keeps its running gear

Only the stock body shell carried the suspensions' and engine's slots, the plates, hitch and nitrous, the reference nodes and the cameras. A configuration on a kit shell (a body variant) spawned as a shell lying on the ground: no wheels, no engine, "Reference nodes missing" in the log. Every version of the shell now carries all of it; a version without the stock shell's reference node names places its own.

## The car weighs what it should

The export gives the body's nodes the weight the game's stiff beams need under them (an engine's mounts, a suspension's). It added that weight for every engine and every suspension that could be chosen at once: a car with fifteen engines and six suspensions had its body weigh 2.7 t, sat on its bump stops and would not pull away. Alternatives of one slot are never fitted together, so per node only the heaviest of them counts now (the Skyline showcase's default configuration went from 3.9 t to 1.85 t).

## Traction control lets the car pull away

The Traction control script counted any wheel movement while the car stood still as wheelspin and cut the throttle to 15%: with a modest engine the car sat at 2,500 rpm on a slipping clutch and never moved. Wheels turning slower than 3 m/s are a launch now, and the cut never goes below 30%. Re-add the script (or regenerate the mod) to get the new one.

## Under the hood

The showcase build's plan can set a part's target mass (the Skyline's shell), and the Skyline's seat textures are written as real PNGs.
