# JBeam Forge 0.19.1: animated parts can be taken off again

## Downloads

- **JBeam-Forge-Setup-0.19.1.exe**: the installer. It updates any earlier version in place; your settings, projects and library stay.
- **JBeam-Forge-0.19.1-portable.exe**: a single exe, nothing to install.
- **jbeam-forge_0.19.1_amd64.deb** and **JBeam-Forge-0.19.1.AppImage**: Linux.
- **jbeam_forge_blender.py**: the Blender add-on. **jbeam_forge_ingame.zip**: JBeam Forge for inside the game (the app installs it for you).

Projects stay on format v28.

## Fixed

- An animated part (a steering wheel, needle or pedal under Animated parts in the Moving parts workspace) looked like it could not be removed: the only way was a Stop animating button at the very bottom of its settings, below the fold on a short window. Every row in the Moving parts list now has an × (animated parts and scripted movement both), and the Animation section's bin sits in its header.

Please report what breaks: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Connor-Moyle/jbeam-forge---Beam-NG-Car-creation-Tool/issues).
